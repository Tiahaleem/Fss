Payments & Refunds

We accept all major debit cards, bank transfers and mobile wallets, processed securely through Paystack and Flutterwave. We never store your card details on our servers.
Yes — bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 6 hours before departure from your dashboard. See our Refund Policy for the specific timeframes and amounts.
Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method. Bank transfer refunds may take a little longer depending on your bank.
Yes. All payments run through PCI-compliant processors (Paystack and Flutterwave). FSS Transport never sees or stores your full card number.
