Refund Policy

Clear timeframes for bus ticket and courier refunds, and how long they take to process.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Bus Ticket Cancellations

Refund eligibility for bus tickets depends on how far ahead of departure you cancel:

  • More than 6 hours before departure: full refund, minus a small processing fee.
  • Less than 6 hours before departure: not eligible for a cash refund, but may be converted to travel credit at our discretion.
  • No-show (missed departure): not eligible for a refund or credit.

2. Delays & Service Disruptions

If your trip is delayed by more than 90 minutes due to a cause within our control, or cancelled by FSS Transport for any reason, you're entitled to a full refund or a free rebooking onto the next available departure, whichever you prefer.

3. Courier & Parcel Refunds

If a parcel is lost or arrives damaged due to our handling, we'll refund the shipping fee and compensate up to the declared value you provided at booking, capped at ₦500,000 per parcel. Refunds for cancelled courier pickups follow the same timing rules as bus tickets above, measured from the scheduled pickup time.

4. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Paystack or Flutterwave, and typically arrive within 3–5 business days. Bank transfer refunds can occasionally take a little longer depending on your bank's own processing times — this part is outside our control.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • Payment processor transaction fees, where applicable.
  • Any portion of a trip already completed (e.g. cancelling partway through a multi-stop journey).
  • Items declined for courier shipment due to prohibited contents.

6. How to Request a Refund

Cancel directly from your dashboard where possible — this is the fastest route. For anything the dashboard can't handle, contact support with your booking or tracking reference and we'll process it manually.

7. Contact Us

Refund questions can be sent to hello@fss.ng or by calling +234 800 123 4567.
