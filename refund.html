Refund Policy

Clear timeframes for bus ticket and courier refunds, and how long they take to process.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Bus Ticket Cancellations

All bus ticket bookings are non-refundable once cancelled by the passenger, regardless of how far in advance the cancellation is made. Please make sure of your travel date and details before booking.

2. Delays & Service Disruptions

If your trip is delayed by more than 90 minutes due to a cause within our control, or cancelled by FSS Transport for any reason, you're entitled to a full refund or a free rebooking onto the next available departure, whichever you prefer. This protection applies only when FSS Transport is responsible for the disruption — it does not apply to a passenger's own decision to cancel.

3. Courier & Parcel Refunds

If a parcel is lost or arrives damaged due to our handling, we'll refund the shipping fee and compensate up to the declared value you provided at booking, capped at ₦500,000 per parcel. A courier pickup cancelled by the sender is non-refundable, the same as a bus ticket cancellation above.

4. How Approved Refunds Are Processed

Where a refund genuinely applies under Sections 2 or 3 above, it's returned to your original payment method via Paystack or Flutterwave, and typically arrives within 3–5 business days. Bank transfer refunds can occasionally take a little longer depending on your bank's own processing times — this part is outside our control.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • Any booking cancelled by the passenger or sender themselves.
  • Payment processor transaction fees, where applicable.
  • Any portion of a trip already completed (e.g. cancelling partway through a multi-stop journey).
  • Items declined for courier shipment due to prohibited contents.
