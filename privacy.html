Privacy Policy

How FSS Transport collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you book a trip, request a courier quote, or contact support, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, government ID type (for boarding verification), payment confirmation details, and — for courier bookings — sender and recipient details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process bookings, issue e-tickets, and confirm courier pickups and deliveries.
  • To send SMS and email updates about your trip or parcel status.
  • To verify your identity at boarding and prevent fraud.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To improve our routes, pricing, and service quality.

3. Sharing of Information

We share the minimum information necessary with trusted third parties who help us operate our services, including payment processors (Paystack, Flutterwave), SMS/email delivery providers, and terminal staff for boarding verification. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, and we continuously work to strengthen our safeguards.

5. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and its regulations, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legitimate business and legal record-keeping requirements (such as booking history for dispute resolution).

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users aged 18 and above. Minors may travel as passengers under a booking made by an adult, but accounts are not created in a minor's name.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, reach us at hello@fss.ng or +234 800 123 4567.
